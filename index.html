Joo-Won Hong

Postdoctoral Researcher

IUSTI, Aix-Marseille Université - CNRS

I’m a postdoc at IUSTI (Marseille, France) working on tunable kirigami structures in a flow.

I did my PhD student at the PMMH (ESPCI-PSL/CNRS) on shape morphing structures.
Before that, I finished my engineering degree in material sciences at Polytech Sorbonne. During my studies I completed two internships at IBPS and Saint-Gobain Research Paris.

I’m passionate about architecture and biomimicry, I hope these fields will serve as a key inspirations for my future research.

PhD Thesis

I did my PhD at PMMH in the MecaWet team supervised by Benoît Roman, José Bico and Étienne Reyssat.

My thesis was about programming morphable metamaterials. Focusing on deployable structures, particularly Kirigami and Gridshells.
